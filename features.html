Quizzes

Timed multiple-choice questions at the end of each part. You find out straight away if you got it right, and if not, you get another go.

◈

Puzzles

Match kings and monuments to their names, and put events in the right order on a timeline.

♛

Stars and badges

Earn stars and badges as you finish levels. Clear a level and the next one unlocks.

අ

Three languages

Play the whole game in Sinhala, Tamil or English, and switch at any time from the menu.

Saves your progress

Your progress is saved to your account, so you can stop and carry on from where you left off, even on a different PC.

Made for PC

Built for the computers in your school lab and the one at home. Everything works with a mouse and keyboard.

How it works

Four steps to start playing.

It only takes a couple of minutes to get going.

  1. 1

    Pick your language

    Choose Sinhala, Tamil or English on the first screen.

  2. 2

    Choose your grade

    Pick Grade 7, 8 or 9 to open the chapter that matches your class.

  3. 3

    Play and learn

    Visit each place in the chapter, answer the quiz questions and solve the puzzles.

  4. 4

    Earn stars and badges

    Collect stars and badges as you go. Finish a level and the next one opens up.
