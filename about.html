Why we're building it

What we're working towards.

WHO IT IS FOR

Grade 7 to 9 students.

Students in Sri Lanka who study history in Sinhala, Tamil or English and want a better way to remember it than reading the textbook again.

WHY WE'RE MAKING IT

History should stick.

In our survey of 200 students, 27 said they learn history mainly for the exam and 27 said they struggle to remember the dates. We think playing through it can help.

WHERE WE ARE NOW

Prototype done.

The screens are designed in Figma and we are building the first chapter now. Join the waitlist to hear when you can try it.
