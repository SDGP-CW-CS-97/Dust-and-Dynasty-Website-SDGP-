The chapters

A chapter for every grade.

Each grade gets its own chapter, built around what you study in class that year. We are still checking the topics against the syllabus, so they may change.

GRADE 7 / CHAPTER I

Kings of Anuradhapura

Meet the kings from your textbook, like Devanampiya Tissa and Dutugemunu, and the cities and monuments they left behind.

How a chapter works

Kingdoms and tiers.

These screens are from our Figma prototype, so some things may change before launch.

YOUR KINGDOM

Join a kingdom first.

Before your first chapter you join Eldoria, Drakemoor or Sylvaris. Every point you earn after that adds to your kingdom's total.

BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD

Three tiers in every chapter.

Each chapter has three parts: The Beginning, Lost Secrets and Royal Legacy. Clear all three at Bronze, then come back for Silver and Gold.

Inside a chapter

Put what you learn
into practice.

These are the activities we are planning for the chapters. Each one gives you a different way to work through the lesson.

Answer a few questions.

Try short quizzes about the people, places and events you have just explored.

Find what belongs together.

Match names, places and facts from the chapter. Think about how they connect, rather than learning each one on its own.

Put the story in order.

Arrange events in the order they happened. Work out what came first and what followed.

Getting started

How your journey begins.

Choose your grade, work through a chapter and see what you remember.

01 / CHOOSE

Choose your grade.

Pick Grade 7, 8 or 9 to find the chapters for your class.

02 / EXPLORE

Play through a chapter.

Explore historical places, answer questions and solve puzzles based on what you learn.

03 / PROGRESS

Keep going.

Collect stars and badges as you finish chapters. Your progress is saved to your account so you can carry on later.
