Inside a chapter

Put what you learn
into practice.

These are the activities we are planning for the chapters. Each one gives you a different way to work through the lesson.

Answer a few questions.

Try short quizzes about the people, places and events you have just explored.

Find what belongs together.

Match names, places and facts from the chapter. Think about how they connect, rather than learning each one on its own.

Put the story in order.

Arrange events in the order they happened. Work out what came first and what followed.
