How a chapter works

Kingdoms and tiers.

These screens are from our Figma prototype, so some things may change before launch.

YOUR KINGDOM

Join a kingdom first.

Before your first chapter you join Eldoria, Drakemoor or Sylvaris. Every point you earn after that adds to your kingdom's total.

BRONZE, SILVER, GOLD

Three tiers in every chapter.

Each chapter has three parts: The Beginning, Lost Secrets and Royal Legacy. Clear all three at Bronze, then come back for Silver and Gold.

Inside a chapter

What you do in
each chapter.

Every chapter mixes three kinds of activities, so it's not just question after question.

Answer a few questions.

Short quizzes on the people, places and events you've just seen.

Find what belongs together.

Match kings, places and monuments from the chapter, like which king built which stupa.

Put the story in order.

Put events on a timeline in the order they happened.

Getting started

How your journey begins.

Choose your grade, work through a chapter and see what you remember.

01 / CHOOSE

Choose your grade.

Pick Grade 7, 8 or 9 to find the chapters for your class.

02 / EXPLORE

Play through a chapter.

Explore historical places, answer questions and solve puzzles based on what you learn.

03 / PROGRESS

Keep going.

Collect stars and badges as you finish chapters. Your progress is saved to your account so you can carry on later.
