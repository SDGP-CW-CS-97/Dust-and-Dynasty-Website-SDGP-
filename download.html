Before you play

What you'll need.

Nothing fancy. We'll post the exact system requirements here before launch.

01 / A PC

A desktop or laptop.

A normal PC is enough, like the ones in your school computer lab or the one you use at home.

02 / A BROWSER

Chrome, Firefox or Edge.

The game runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to download or install.

03 / INTERNET

An internet connection.

You need to be online to log in, save your progress and add points to your kingdom.

04 / CONTROLS

A mouse and keyboard.

That's all you need to play. Everything in the game works with them.

FAQ

Questions you might have.

When is it coming out?

We don't have a date yet. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as you can try it.

Which grades is it for?

Grades 7, 8 and 9. Each grade has its own chapter based on what you study in class.

Can I play it on my phone?

Not right now. We're designing it for PC screens first, like the computers in your school lab and laptops at home.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You pick a dynast name and a password, and add an email or phone number so we can verify your account. We never ask for your real name, and your contact details are never shown to other players.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your web browser, so you just open the site and play.

Which languages can I play in?

Sinhala, Tamil and English. You pick one when you start and can switch at any time.

Who is making it?

Five Computer Science students at IIT Sri Lanka. You can meet us on the About page.
